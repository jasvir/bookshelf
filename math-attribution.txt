MATHEMATICAL SHELF CONSTRUCTION

The finite Hat polygon fixture in hat-tiles.js is derived from the initial H
and P metatiles and their first attachment in the authors' Hatviz construction.
Its six congruent Hat tiles include one reflected tile. The patch is stored as
exact triangular-lattice coordinates and rendered with uniform scaling. It is
a finite illustration of the construction, not an infinite tiling generated by
this webpage. Checks confirmed equal tile areas, matching interior seams, no
overlapping interiors, and a single exterior boundary with no interior holes.

Mathematical work: David Smith, Joseph Samuel Myers, Craig S. Kaplan, and
Chaim Goodman-Strauss, "An aperiodic monotile" (2023; published 2024).
Primary project page: https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/hat/
Construction source: https://github.com/isohedral/hatviz
Source files: geometry.js and hat.js, initial H/P definitions and constructPatch.
